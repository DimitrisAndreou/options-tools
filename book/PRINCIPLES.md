# Principles for writing passages for the book, for the benefit of the AI agent.

Each paragraph serves a single purpose. It nails it efficiently and moves on to the next.
Concepts are illustrated with minimal examples. Symmetries are exploited to simplify explanations.
Keep it short, sharp, direct. 

Concepts build on the ones before. Chapters build from the concepts of previous chapters.

I, the author, shall present goals, including paths of goals (first we hit this goal, then that etc),
and the agent will help find minimal prose that connects the dots in an effective manner.

## What we believe:

We adopt Mises & Rothbard's praxeological approach to the economy. This is the foundation, but then we
switch focus to market mechanics.

Money is just another good, it's not a measure of value. Still, it's the best tool we have for economic calculation,
and for estimating PnL. The base rate should not be considered "profit", but people typically do, especially the taxman.

Trade is mutually beneficial, but from a pure money perspective, it's a zero sum game.

Options can be decomposed to simpler ideas and understood more fundamentally.

Covered calls can be understood at a more basic level, similar to how the accompanying tool shows.

Generally nobody can have an edge. Market implied probabilities are objective.

Market data > statistical models.

We can understand options by abstracting away from conceptually unnecessary complications (lot size, settlement type).

Once we have built fundamental understanding, we can reintroduce the extra complexities of the real world.

Put-call parity: show how options and futures are bridged. How futures and spot markets are bridged, interest rates, arbitrages.

## Judge the book by these questions

- Is every concept understandable by concepts that were properly introduced previously?

- Is the signal-to-noise ratio high?

- Is it boring / expected? Or takes the reader through a novel, interesting, revealing journey?

- Dangling references? Promises made in the book (we will examine this later / at that chapter) not kept?
