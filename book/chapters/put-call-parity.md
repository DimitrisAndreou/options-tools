Construct future via options. Put-call parity. Simplified formula.

Another way to derive Put-Call Parity is via the Fair Game law.

Spot price is the expected value of the asset (discounted to present).
Forward price is the same, but in the future.
The "upside" equals the "downside", since they need to average to the forward price (otherwise the average would move).
The ATM call captures the upside, and it must equal the ATM put (downside).
