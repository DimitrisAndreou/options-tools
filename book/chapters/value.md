# Value

How do you prefer A to B? Subjective.
But if there's a market, you can tell whether some quantity of A is more or less valuable than a quantity of B. The ratio is the price.
If your available choice implies a price that is better than the market, you just take it, and go back to the market. You end up with more of what you actually wanted.
